# T15 · Synthetic company: Wattlebrook Energy

- **Model**: sol
- **Tier**: normal
- **Depends on**: T03 (checks), T04/T05 (parser, parts loader) — all merged
- **Goal**: A deterministic generator that writes Wattlebrook Energy's pack — an annual report PDF that contains the sustainability report as a page-range part, a separate climate data supplement PDF, `parts.yaml` and a fully known answer key — with planted issues and difficulty rules, and tests that parse the generated PDFs with our own parser and prove the answer key is right.
- **Design**: `02-design.md` §5 (page convention, parts, two-page spreads, tables), §13 (answer key fields, synthetic company); decisions D55, D64, D68, D69, D70, D71, D74, D76; `01-requirements.md` checks list and inconsistency types T1–T6
- **Libraries**: `reportlab` (new; in the stack table, T15 only), `pyyaml`, `pymupdf` (tests only, through `cda.parsing`), pydantic. `types-reportlab` may be added as a dev dependency if mypy needs it; otherwise a mypy override limited to `reportlab.*`.

## Files
Only these may be created or changed:
- `src/cda/synth/__init__.py`, `src/cda/synth/wattlebrook.py` (build + CLI), and further modules under `src/cda/synth/` (for example `content.py` for the text, `answer_key.py` for the key model) — all new
- `data/synthetic/wattlebrook/`: `wattlebrook-annual-report-2026.pdf`, `wattlebrook-climate-data-supplement-2026.pdf`, `parts.yaml`, `answer_key.yaml`, `README.md` (all generated by the CLI except the README, all committed — the company is fictional, D72 does not apply)
- `tests/synth/` (new)
- `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock` (add `reportlab`)

## Entry point
`build_pack(out_dir: Path, *, s5_position: Literal["start", "middle", "end"] = "middle") -> PackManifest`
and CLI `uv run python -m cda.synth.wattlebrook --out data/synthetic/wattlebrook [--s5-position start|middle|end]`.

- Fully deterministic: no randomness (or a fixed seed), reportlab `invariant=True`, fixed metadata. Two builds give byte-identical PDFs and identical YAML.
- `PackManifest` is returned in memory (not written): for every tagged paragraph/table row the generator records `document`, `pdf_page`, column (`left`/`right`/`single`), printed page, and the section heading it sits under. Tests use it; the answer key is derived from it.
- Page numbers in `parts.yaml`, the answer key and the AASB S2 index table are **computed from the build**, never typed by hand (two-pass build or reportlab `afterFlowable` hooks; the second pass must not move pages).
- Default output (`middle`) is the committed pack. Other positions are for experiment E6 (D68) and are written only where `--out` points; they are not committed.

## The company (fictional; no real company, person or facility names)
Wattlebrook Energy Limited (ASX: WBE), a generator and electricity retailer in NSW and Queensland, about 410,000 retail customers. Assets: **Tarrawong Power Station** (gas, 680 MW, Hunter Valley NSW, units 1–4), **Bellbird Ridge Wind Farm** (310 MW, Queensland), **Coomba Battery** (200 MW, NSW). Financial year FY2026 = year ended 30 June 2026. This is Wattlebrook's **first** AASB S2 reporting period (1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026).

All numbers below are the only values of these quantities anywhere in the pack. Filler text must not introduce any other emissions figure, target, temperature, scenario, weather event or cross-reference.

## Document structure (D76, D70)
A4 portrait, standard Type 1 fonts (Helvetica family), real text layer.

**`wattlebrook-annual-report-2026.pdf`** (`documents.kind = annual`), about 36–50 PDF pages:
1. Cover (pdf page 1, no page number) and contents (pdf page 2, no number).
2. "Our year at a glance": **one two-page spread** — a single landscape PDF page (clearly wider than tall) carrying printed pages 1 (left half) and 2 (right half); operational highlights only, **no evidence for any check** and none of the quantities above. From pdf page 4 onward the printed page is `pdf_page − 1`.
3. Chair and CEO letter; Operating and financial review (OFR, two columns); Material business risks; Directors' report (with Environmental regulation, committee attendance table, board skills matrix table); Remuneration report (with a scorecard table).
4. **Climate-related financial disclosures** — the sustainability report, a contiguous run of about 12–16 pages, two-column body, own headings (Governance, Strategy, Risk management, Metrics and targets, Important information about this section, AASB S2 index). This page range is the `sustainability` part in `parts.yaml` (label `Climate-related financial disclosures`); its first page starts with that heading.
5. Financial statements and notes (single column, tables), Directors' declaration, Shareholder information.

Running header on every page except cover and spread: `Wattlebrook Energy Limited | Annual Report 2026` at the same position; footer with the printed page number. The supplement has its own header `Wattlebrook Climate Data Supplement 2026` and printed page = pdf page.

**`wattlebrook-climate-data-supplement-2026.pdf`** (`documents.kind = other`), about 6–10 pages, single column: data tables, methodology, glossary.

Tables use full grid lines (outer box and all inner lines) so `pymupdf.find_tables` sees them. Evidence paragraphs are kept on one page and inside one column (`KeepTogether` or equivalent) so a quote never straddles a column or page break. Body prose is plausible and non-repetitive (no lorem ipsum), written in Australian English.

## Difficulty rules (enforced by tests)
- **D1 paraphrase**: no evidence quote whose difficulty is not `direct` contains any 3-word sequence (lower-case, punctuation removed, after dropping the stopwords `a an and the of to in for on by with its our their is are be`) that also occurs in the check's `title` or `queries` in `checks.yaml`.
- **D2 mix**: at least 1 `not_found` cell; at least 3 cells each labelled `paraphrase` and `split`, at least 2 `table`, at least 1 `direct`; every one of the 15 checks appears exactly once.
- **D3 distractors**: each distractor in the table above exists in the named place and is never listed as evidence.
- **D4 true absence**: for every `not_found` cell and every planted one-sided issue the answer key lists `scanned` ranges covering every page where evidence could be (whole SR, AR, SUP).

## Answer key (`answer_key.yaml`, D71)
```yaml
key_version: 1
company: Wattlebrook Energy Limited
labeler: generator
documents:
  - {filename: wattlebrook-annual-report-2026.pdf, kind: annual}
  - {filename: wattlebrook-climate-data-supplement-2026.pdf, kind: other}
cells:
  - check_id: M1
    status: inconsistent            # found | partial | not_found | inconsistent | not_applicable
    relief_applied: scope3_first_year   # or null
    difficulty: split
    unsure: false
    evidence:
      - {document: ..., part: sustainability, pdf_page: 27, quote: "...", role: primary, source_type: text, difficulty: paraphrase}
    scanned:                        # required for not_found / inconsistent
      - {document: ..., first_pdf_page: 1, last_pdf_page: 44}
planted_issues:
  - {id: t3_scope1, type: T3, check_id: M1, locations: [{document, part, pdf_page, quote}, ...]}
  # types: T1, T3, T4, T5, disclaimer, single_scenario, injection
```
`part` is the resolved kind (`sustainability` for pages in the part, otherwise the document's kind). `quote` is verbatim text of the generated PDF, short enough to sit inside one unit (one sentence or one table cell phrase). Validate the file with a Pydantic model in `src/cda/synth/`; T18 will move or reuse it.

## Tests to write first (`tests/synth/`)
Build the pack once per session into a tmp dir (session fixture) and parse both PDFs with `cda.parsing.pdf.parse_pdf`.
1. Determinism: two builds → byte-identical PDFs and identical YAML; the committed files in `data/synthetic/wattlebrook/` equal a fresh `middle` build (drift test).
2. `parts.yaml` loads with `cda.parsing.parts.load_parts_file`; the part's first page is the page with the "Climate-related financial disclosures" heading and its last page is the AASB S2 index page.
3. Every answer-key evidence quote (after `cda.parsing.units.normalize_text`) is a substring of a parsed unit on the stated `pdf_page`; `source_type: table` quotes are inside a `table_row` unit; the part resolved with `cda.parsing.parts.resolve_part` equals the key's `part`. Same for every `planted_issues` location.
4. Layout: the running headers never appear in any unit's text; parsed `printed_page` equals `pdf_page − 1` for numbered annual-report pages from pdf page 4 and equals `pdf_page` in the supplement; the spread page yields units with `page_half` `left` and `right`; on every two-column page, manifest paragraphs of the left column come before those of the right column in parse order.
5. Tables: the S1 risk table, the SUP emissions table and the AR scorecard each parse into a `table` parent with one `table_row` child per data row.
6. Planted-issue invariants on parsed text: no unit in the part matches `flood|inundat|storm|cyclone|heavy rain|extreme weather|severe weather` (case-insensitive) while AR has the flood loss; every `°C` value in the pack is below 2 and none of the high-warming scenario names occur; FY2026 Scope 1 appears as 1,842,600 in SR and SUP and as 1.91 Mt only in AR; "Safeguard" occurs only in AR outside the part; the disclaimer is in the part; both injection passages are present and are not evidence in any cell.
7. Difficulty rules D1–D4.
8. The AASB S2 index rows point to the printed pages where the referenced headings actually are.
9. `s5_position` start and end: the S5 evidence moves (start: before the part's first page; end: after the financial statements), the answer key follows, tests 3 and 6 still pass for both variants.
10. DB test (skipped without Docker, like the others): ingest both PDFs with `cda.parsing.store.ingest_pdf`, apply `parts.yaml` with `replace_parts`, and check each answer-key evidence page resolves to the expected part through the stored rows.
